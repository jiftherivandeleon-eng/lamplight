Your notes, answered privately.

Lamplight is a free Windows app. Add your notes, PDFs, slides and pictures, then ask questions or build study guides. The AI runs on your own computer, so your files never leave it.

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · no account needed

Three steps

No settings, no command line.

  1. Download and installOpen the file you downloaded and click through the installer. A Lamplight icon appears on your desktop.
  2. First-time setupLamplight sets up its AI engine and downloads the AI once. It shows you the progress. This needs internet and a few gigabytes.
  3. Start askingDrop in your files and ask. After setup it works offline.

What you can do

Built for studying from your own material.

Ask your documents

Pick a file and ask questions. Answers say where they came from, and everyday chat works too.

Study guides

Turn a whole document into notes with a question-and-answer key.

Pictures too

Photos of handwritten or printed notes are read for you. Voice recordings are coming in a later version.

Private by design

Everything stays on your computer. Only the optional Cloud mode sends text out, and it asks first.

Good to know before you install

  • Windows may warn you. The app isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it's from an unknown publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To be sure you have the real file, compare its checksum on the release page.
  • It needs some room. Plan for about 10 GB of free disk space and 8 GB of memory. A graphics card helps, but isn't required.
  • A small AI can be wrong. Check important answers against your own sources.
  • Windows only for now. Phone and Mac versions aren't available yet.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license. The AI models are downloaded from Ollama and have their own terms.

Does it send my files anywhere?

No. Local mode runs entirely on your computer. Cloud mode is optional, needs a login stored on your PC, and sends your question and the selected document's text to Ollama's servers.

Does it need internet?

Only for the first-time setup and for Cloud mode. Normal use works offline.

Where are my files kept?

In your user folder, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lamplight. Uninstalling the app leaves them there, so nothing is lost by accident.

How do I know the download is genuine?

Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum. In PowerShell run Get-FileHash .\LamplightSetup.exe and compare.
